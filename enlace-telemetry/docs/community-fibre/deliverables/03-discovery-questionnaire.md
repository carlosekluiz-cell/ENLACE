# Network Operations Discovery
### Community Fibre × Enlace pilot
*Pulso Technologies Limited · enlace.network*

---

Before we run the free pilot, we'd like to understand how Community Fibre's network operations
actually run day to day — so we tailor what Enlace looks for to your real priorities, and so
anything we show you is grounded in your operation rather than industry averages.

It takes ~15 minutes. Ranges and estimates are completely fine, and skip anything commercially
sensitive. There are no wrong answers — this is discovery, not an audit.

---

## 1 · A day in network operations

1. Walk us through a typical day for your NOC / network operations team. What do they check first each morning?
2. How is the team structured — network operations vs field engineering — and what's the out-of-hours / on-call setup?
3. When you mention **"Mission Control"** — is that your NOC, a specific dashboard, a platform? What does an operator actually see on it today?
4. When something goes wrong, how do you usually find out first — an alarm, a dashboard, a customer call, a wholesale partner, or social media?

## 2 · Visibility & monitoring today

5. What tooling do you use for network / PON monitoring today? (e.g. Adtran Mosaic / Mosaic One, a general NMS, in-house, Elasticsearch/Kibana, OTDR/fibre monitoring.)
6. You mentioned new **AI systems rolling out through 2026** — is that Adtran's Mosaic One / Clarity? What do you expect it to give you, and where do you think it might fall short?
7. Roughly what share of issues do you catch **before** the first customer call — i.e. how much of your monitoring is predictive vs reactive?
8. Can you see **per-ONT optical signal (Rx/Tx power) trends over time** today, or mainly current status and alarms?
9. How many distinct OLT / equipment vendors are in your estate, and is that changing as you grow?
10. Where does your telemetry / alarm data live — vendor cloud, your own infrastructure, or both? How do you feel about telemetry staying on-prem vs leaving your network?

## 3 · Faults & field dispatch

11. Roughly how many **engineer visits / truck rolls** per month, per 1,000 connected subscribers?
12. What's your rough **fully-loaded cost per engineer visit** (labour, travel, vehicle, parts)?
13. What share of dispatches are **"no fault found"** or end in a repeat / second visit?
14. What are your **top recurring physical fault modes**? (e.g. a rogue/reflecting ONT taking down a whole PON tree, moisture in splice enclosures, dirty connectors, macro-bends, ageing SFPs, fibre cuts.)
15. When a fault hits, how do you **locate it physically** today, and how long does it typically take to get a technician to the right spot (mean time to repair)?

## 4 · Customers & churn

16. What's your annual **churn rate**, and how much do you attribute to service quality / reliability vs price or competition?
17. What's your blended **ARPU** and rough **cost to acquire** a subscriber?
18. What are your most common **support-call themes**, and roughly what does a call / ticket cost you?
19. With the **VodafoneThree wholesale** relationship, do undiagnosed faults now carry SLA penalties or reputational exposure? Has that changed your priorities?

## 5 · Alarms, tickets & automation

20. Roughly how many **alarms per day** does the NOC see, and what share are actionable vs noise? Is alarm fatigue a real issue?
21. How are tickets created and routed today — manual, or auto-generated from monitoring? What ticketing / on-call tooling do you use (ServiceNow, Jira, PagerDuty, Opsgenie, in-house)?
22. How is the field team dispatched today — and would **automated alerts to the team on WhatsApp, with a map pin to the fault location**, be useful or unwelcome?
23. If telemetry could open a **planned-maintenance** ticket for a splice that's *going* to fail in a few weeks — before any customer is affected — where would that sit on your priority list?

## 6 · Success & the pilot

24. If a pilot worked, what would make you say "yes, this is worth keeping"? Which single metric would you most want to move — truck rolls, MTTR, churn, first-call resolution, or alarm noise?
25. For the free pilot, could you export **~a month of ONT / optical telemetry** (CSV, or via NETCONF / Mosaic) from **one area where you've had recurring issues**? That's all we need to show you what Enlace finds in your real data — nothing touches your network.
