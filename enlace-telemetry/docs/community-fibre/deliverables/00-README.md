# Community Fibre — Deliverable Pack

Customer-facing documents for the Enlace pilot. These are written *for Community Fibre* (the internal
strategy/meeting prep lives one level up in `../`). Send or present in this order.

| # | Document | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Pilot proposal** | The lead document — the whole story in two pages; links to the rest |
| 02 | **UK FTTP market dossier** | "From building to operating" — the market case, why now (the icing on the cake) |
| 03 | **Discovery questionnaire** | Send / walk through with their ops owner; gathers the ROI inputs |
| 04 | **Beyond-monitoring vision** | The closed-loop story: auto-ticket → WhatsApp + map pin → proactive maintenance |
| 05 | **Technical differentiation** | For their engineers: where Enlace sits, why Rust, what's built — with honest caveats |
| 06 | **Pilot plan & data request** | Exactly how the free pilot runs and what telemetry export we need |

## How they fit together
- **Lead with 01** (proposal) + **02** (market dossier) to establish *why now*.
- **03** (questionnaire) gathers their real numbers so nothing is overclaimed.
- **04 / 05** go deeper for the operations and engineering audiences respectively.
- **06** closes on the concrete, low-risk ask: one month of telemetry from one problem area.

## Guardrails baked into every doc (do not break)
- **No pricing** — free pilot, preferential pricing at launch.
- **Complement Adtran, never compete** — self-hosted, no per-device licence, vendor-agnostic.
- **Don't claim "nothing exists"** — we win on the *intersection*; name NetSense/PBN if pushed.
- **Validation honesty** — 10/10 blind test = synthetic; 98% = design accuracy; the pilot validates on real data.
- **"Billions/day" = fleet aggregate**, always shown with the arithmetic.
- **Brazil = "20,000+ regional ISPs"**, not 13,000.
- **Read-only** is a headline, not a footnote.

*Internal strategy, the Adtran/"Mission Control" decode, and the meeting playbook are in `../01`–`../05`.*
